# ExoticLuxSports
A program that simulates the car buying experience but with luxury cars. The use of Linked Lists and Prioirty Queue is included in the program

I designed a vehicle inventory management system that organises and stores inventory while making affordable ones come to the front.

The use of Priority Queue to simulate a premium customer service by prioritizing customers based on income and potential purchase value.

It was a really fun and interesting project that was assigned for me to do. The professor gave me freedom to chooose any topic and with my broad knowlegde of cars, i said why not.

Using what i learned in this Data Structures course, i was able to bring the car buying experience right to you.
